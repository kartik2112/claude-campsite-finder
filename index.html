How to get a site

  1. Popular coast camps (Kirk Creek, Steep Ravine, Kirby Cove, Pfeiffer) fill 6 months ahead. Check for cancellations in the morning and at night.
  2. Walk-up (first-come) sites go early. Arrive on Friday or before 11 AM. Bring cash for the pay box.
  3. Private Big Sur camps (Ventana, Big Sur Campground, Riverside, Fernwood) cannot be checked here. Use their booking pages.
  4. Hwy 1 through Big Sur is open. Check Caltrans QuickMap after rain.
  5. Late-fall nights are cold: about 35–45 °F at the coast. Bring a warm sleeping bag.
  6. Check the campground page for fire bans before you light a fire.
